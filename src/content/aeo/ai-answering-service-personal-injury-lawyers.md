## What an AI answering service should do for a PI firm

An AI answering service should respond promptly, collect useful intake details, and route urgent matters to the firm. For a personal injury practice, slow or missed responses can mean a potential client contacts another firm first.

Check which channels the service handles. Some tools focus on phone calls, while others can respond to missed calls, web forms, and texts too. Confirm whether appointments can be booked into the calendar your team uses. Boltcall answers inbound calls, missed calls, web forms, and texts within seconds, 24/7, and books into Google Calendar and Cal.com.

A fast response should not be confused with legal screening. See [how law firms can respond to new leads faster](/blog/law-firm-respond-new-leads-faster/) and [why response time affects whether a business gets the job](/blog/does-response-time-affect-whether-local-business-gets-job/).

## Intake safeguards and handoffs

An AI intake service should make clear what it can and cannot do, and should send urgent matters to the firm. Boltcall opens each call by identifying itself as an AI assistant and saying the call is recorded. It collects conflict-check names first and tells callers that the call does not make them a client.

Boltcall flags urgent matters such as custody, arrest, a court date within 48 hours, deportation, or imminent deadlines, then alerts the team. It does intake only and does not decide whether a caller has a viable claim. Boltcall has no native Clio, MyCase, or Filevine integration; other tools can connect through Zapier, Make, and webhooks.
