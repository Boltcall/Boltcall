# AI intake that books consultations for a personal injury firm

**Short answer:** AI intake that books consultations for a personal injury firm can answer new inquiries around the clock, collect initial information, and schedule a consultation. Boltcall answers inbound calls, missed calls, web forms, and texts within seconds, in English and Spanish. ## What AI intake handles for a personal injury firm

AI intake for a personal injury firm can respond to new inquiries from phone calls, missed calls, web forms, and texts, then collect information and book a consultation. Boltcall responds to these channels within seconds, 24/7, in English and Spanish.

A fast response matters when someone is contacting more than one firm. A prompt answer gives the firm an opportunity to speak with the person while they are actively seeking help. See [how law firms can respond to new leads faster](/blog/law-firm-respond-new-leads-faster/).

The assistant should make its role clear. Boltcall opens every call by saying it is an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. Its role is intake only: it does not give legal advice, assess a case, predict case value, or quote fees.

## How consultation booking works

Boltcall books consultations into Google Calendar and Cal.com. It can connect with other tools through Zapier, Make, and webhooks, but it does not have a native Clio, MyCase, or Filevine integration.

For a personal injury firm, confirm which calendar the firm uses before choosing an intake tool. If the firm relies on Clio, MyCase, or Filevine, account for the lack of a native Boltcall integration and check whether a supported automation route meets the firm’s needs.

A booking system should also make clear what happens after a caller shares information. Boltcall collects conflict-check names first and tells the caller that the call does not make them a client. That distinction helps keep an initial inquiry separate from an attorney-client relationship.

## Personal injury intake should flag urgent matters

An AI intake assistant should alert the legal team when an inquiry involves an urgent deadline or other time-sensitive issue. Boltcall flags urgent matters, including imminent deadlines and court dates within 48 hours, and alerts the team.

The assistant should not make legal judgments while collecting information. Boltcall does not decide whether an injury claim is valid, estimate what it may be worth, or tell the caller what legal action to take. The firm’s team handles legal review and advice.

When comparing tools, check that the assistant discloses it is AI, explains call recording, collects conflict-check names first, and routes urgent matters to the team. These safeguards are important alongside the basic ability to answer and book.
