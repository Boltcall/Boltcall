## What pricing models do legal answering services use?

Legal answering services use three main models: per minute, a bundle of included minutes with overage, and a flat subscription. The model matters more than the headline rate.

| Model | How you are billed | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Per minute | Talk time, often rounded up in 30 or 60 second increments | Long intake calls cost more, and a busy month can raise the invoice sharply |
| Bundled minutes | A monthly plan with a fixed number of minutes | Overage rates, and minimums that apply even in a slow month |
| Flat subscription | One monthly fee | What is included, and whether any usage cap applies |

Criteria: how the bill scales with call volume, and how predictable it is. ## What should you ask a vendor before you sign?

Ask every vendor the same questions in writing, because price pages rarely show the terms that decide your real cost.

1. How is time billed: per minute, per call or flat? What is the rounding increment?
2. Is there a monthly minimum, an overage rate or a setup fee?
3. What is the contract length, and can you cancel or get a refund?
4. What does the caller hear at the start of the call?
5. Who answers after hours, on weekends and on holidays?
6. How are urgent matters, such as custody, arrest or a near court date, escalated?
7. Does it collect conflict-check names before anything else?
8. What can it say about the law, the case or fees?
9. Which calendar and practice tools does it connect to?

## How do you work out the real monthly cost?

The real monthly cost is the invoice at your busiest month, not the average quote. This is general advice for any provider, not a feature of one product.

- Estimate your monthly inbound calls, including after-hours and weekend calls.
- Multiply by your typical intake call length, then apply the vendor's rounding rule.
- Add setup fees, overage charges and minimums.
- Compare that total with a flat plan, and ask what happens at the top of any cap.
- Weigh it against the value of one signed case that a slow reply would have lost.
