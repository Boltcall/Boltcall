## How should you evaluate missed call recovery providers?

Start by deciding whether you need a text sent after a missed call or a live conversation with the caller. According to [Aira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/missed-call-text-back), most "missed call recovery" products are missed-call text-back tools. They send an SMS when a call goes unanswered and move the conversation to text. They do not call anyone back.

Compare providers on these criteria:

- **Channel:** Does it talk to the caller, or only text them?
- **Speed:** How quickly does the first response go out?
- **Disclosure:** Does the caller know it is automated?
- **Scope:** Does it stay inside intake?
- **Urgency:** Does it flag time-sensitive matters to a person?
- **Booking and integrations:** Which calendars and practice tools connect?
- **Pricing model:** Flat fee, usage-based or per-case?
- **Exit terms:** Is there a refund or trial period?

## What should you ask a missed call recovery vendor before signing?

Ask every vendor the same short list of questions, and get the answers in writing. This is general buying advice, not a feature list for any one product.

1. Does it speak with the caller, or only send a text after the call drops?
2. What does the caller hear first? Check your state's rules on call recording and AI disclosure with your own counsel.
3. Can it stay out of legal advice, case evaluation and fee quotes?
4. How does it handle an urgent caller, and who gets alerted?
5. Which calendar and practice management tools connect directly, and which need a workaround?
6. Is the price flat, usage-based or tied to cases? Is there a refund period?
7. Can you test it yourself by calling at night and submitting a web form?
