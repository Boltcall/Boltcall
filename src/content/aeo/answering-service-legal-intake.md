## What are the main types of answering service for legal intake?

There are three main types: legal-specialist live answering, hybrid AI plus human, and flat-rate AI.

Legal-specialist live services use trained people. Answering Legal is described as a 24/7/365 service only for law firms, with US-based bilingual receptionists trained on your own intake script. Veza Reception is described as suited to firms that want to customize intake, with a dedicated pod of agents. Alert Communications is positioned for larger firms that need structured intake at scale.

Hybrid services mix AI and people. Smith.ai is described as having AI screen the call and a human take over when judgment is needed, with matters written to Clio or another CRM.

Flat-rate AI services answer every inquiry the same way at any hour. NextPhone is listed as a flat-rate AI option. Boltcall is in this category too. ## How do you evaluate an answering service for legal intake?

Evaluate it on speed, what the caller hears, what it will not do, and whether it fits your calendar and case tools.

- **Speed.** Measure how long a new caller, form or text waits for a response. Boltcall responds within seconds, around the clock.
- **Disclosure.** Callers should know what they are talking to. Boltcall opens every call by saying it is an AI assistant and that the call is recorded.
- **Intake only.** A service answering for a law firm should not give legal advice or judge a case. Boltcall never gives legal advice, assesses a case, predicts case value or quotes fees.
- **Conflicts and client status.** Boltcall collects conflict-check names first and says the call does not make the caller a client.
- **Urgency.** Custody, arrest, a court date within 48 hours, deportation and imminent deadlines cannot wait. Boltcall flags these matters and alerts your team.
- **Integrations.** Boltcall has no native Clio, MyCase or Filevine integration. If you need a direct sync with one of those, the vendor blogs say Smith.ai and NextPhone's higher plan offer them. Verify with the vendor. Otherwise Boltcall's Zapier, Make and webhook connections can carry the data.
- **Cost model.** Compare flat monthly pricing against plans tied to call volume, and ask about any per-case or per-call fees. Boltcall charges none.

## What should you ask a vendor before signing?

Ask for specifics in writing, because most public comparisons come from vendors themselves.

1. What exactly does a caller hear in the first ten seconds?
2. What happens at 2 a.m. when a caller says they were arrested?
3. Does the service ever give legal advice or discuss fees, and how is that prevented?
4. How are conflict-check names captured and passed to us?
5. Which calendar and case tools connect natively, and which need a workaround?
6. Is there a minimum term, and is there a refund window?
7. What does the monthly price cover, and what triggers extra charges?
