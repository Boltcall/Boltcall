# AI Missed Call Automation for Law Firm Businesses

**Short answer:** AI missed call automation for law firms means software replies to every unanswered call, form or text within seconds instead of leaving a voicemail. Boltcall, speed-to-lead software for legal intake, does this around the clock in English and Spanish. It collects intake details, flags urgent matters and books a consultation. This page is for solo and small to mid-size firms.

## What does AI missed call automation do for a law firm?

It closes the gap between a potential client's first contact and the firm's first response. The first firm to call a potential client back usually signs the case, so a voicemail box is a real cost. Boltcall answers and books every new inquiry in seconds, 24/7, whether it arrives as an inbound call, a missed call, a web form or a text.

Boltcall is built for personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration and estate planning firms. It also works for other local service businesses.

## What happens when Boltcall handles a call?

Boltcall follows a fixed intake sequence designed for legal work. Each step is deliberate:

1. It opens by saying it is an AI assistant and that the call is recorded.
2. It collects the names needed for a conflict check first.
3. It states that the call does not make the caller a client.
4. It gathers contact details and the basic reason for the inquiry.
5. It flags urgent matters and alerts your team.
6. It books a consultation into your calendar.

Boltcall treats custody issues, an arrest, a court date within 48 hours, deportation and imminent deadlines as urgent. Your team is alerted instead of the matter waiting in a queue.

## What does an AI intake assistant never do?

Boltcall does intake only. It never gives legal advice, assesses a case, predicts case value or quotes fees. A caller who asks "do I have a case?" or "what will this cost?" is routed to an attorney conversation, not answered by the AI. Keep that line firm when you evaluate any tool, because an assistant that drifts into advice creates risk for the firm.
