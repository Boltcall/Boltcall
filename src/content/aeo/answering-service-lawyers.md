## How do you evaluate an answering service for lawyers?

Evaluate an answering service for lawyers on six criteria: speed to answer, channels covered, intake rules, calendar booking, hours and price model. Start with the channels, because many leads now arrive by form or text rather than call. Then check the intake rules. ## What should you ask an answering service vendor before signing?

Ask every vendor the same eight questions and get the answers in writing:

- Who answers, a person or software, and how does the caller know?
- Is the call recorded, and does the caller hear that?
- How fast is the answer at peak times and after hours?
- Which channels are covered: calls, missed calls, forms, texts?
- How are urgent matters such as custody, arrest or a court date within 48 hours flagged, and who is alerted?
- What exactly does the service say about legal advice, fees and case value?
- Which calendar and case tools does it connect to, natively or through automation?
- What is the price model, is there a contract, and can you get your money back?

Boltcall opens every call by saying it is an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. It flags urgent matters and alerts your team.
