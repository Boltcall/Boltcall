# How do I stop missing calls at my small law firm?

**Short answer:** To answer “how do I stop missing calls at my small law firm,” set up reliable coverage for calls your team cannot answer, including after hours, and make sure new inquiries get a prompt callback. A virtual receptionist or AI answering service can take calls, collect intake details and book consultations without adding a full-time receptionist.

## Stop sending unanswered calls to voicemail

A small law firm can reduce missed leads by routing calls to a person or answering service whenever staff are unavailable. Start by identifying when calls go unanswered: during consultations, at lunch, after hours or when several calls arrive at once. Then set call forwarding for those periods instead of relying on voicemail alone.

Missed calls matter because the first firm to call a potential client back usually signs the case. Set a callback owner and a clear process for handling messages that still need attorney or staff review. For more on response timing, see [Does response time affect whether a local business gets the job?](/blog/does-response-time-affect-whether-local-business-gets-job/).

An answering service should collect information and route inquiries, not make legal decisions. Ask what it does with conflict-check names, urgent matters, caller consent and consultation bookings before choosing a service.

## Make sure the intake details reach your team

A reliable call-handling setup needs a clear handoff, not just an answered call. Decide who reviews new inquiries, how urgent matters reach the right person and where consultation appointments should be booked. Test the process with a sample inquiry before routing all calls through it.
