## Why do missed calls cost flooring businesses jobs?

A missed call costs a job because a homeowner or property manager asking for a flooring quote often contacts more than one business. The first one to respond usually gets the estimate visit. Installers are often on site, in a van or in a showroom with a customer, so calls go unanswered at the busiest times.

Evenings and weekends are another gap, since many people shop for flooring after work.
