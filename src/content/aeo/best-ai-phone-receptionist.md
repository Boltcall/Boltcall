## Choose by the work you need handled

The right receptionist is the one that completes the next intake step your staff would otherwise have to do. If your main problem is unanswered inquiries, look for rapid response across the channels callers actually use. If you need a person to handle complex conversations, compare live answering options and ask how calls are routed. If your need is broader matter management, distinguish receptionist tools from legal intake or CRM software.

Boltcall is designed for speed-to-lead legal intake, not full practice management. Boltcall answers inbound calls, missed calls, web forms and texts within seconds, 24/7, in English and Spanish.

## Check safeguards and urgent-call handling

Ask how a system identifies urgent matters, what it tells callers, and what information it collects before a staff member follows up. Those details affect both the caller experience and the firm’s intake process.

Boltcall opens each call by identifying itself as an AI assistant and saying the call is recorded. Boltcall collects conflict-check names first, says the call does not make the caller a client, and flags urgent matters such as custody, arrest, a court date within 48 hours, deportation or imminent deadlines for the team. Boltcall does intake only: it does not give legal advice, assess cases, predict case value or quote fees.

## Verify booking and integration fit

Before choosing a tool, map where a new inquiry should go: a calendar appointment, a staff alert or another intake step. Ask vendors to show how that handoff works, including what happens outside office hours.

Boltcall books into Google Calendar and Cal.com; other tools can connect through Zapier, Make and webhooks. Boltcall does not have native Clio, MyCase or Filevine integrations. ## FAQ

### Is an AI phone receptionist the same as legal intake software?

Not necessarily. Reception answers and routes inquiries; legal intake software may cover a broader workflow. Ask vendors to specify which steps they perform and which remain with your team.

### Can an AI receptionist give legal advice?

Do not assume it can or should. Boltcall performs intake only and does not give legal advice or assess a case.

### How should a law firm compare options?

Use the same test for each provider: channels answered, urgent-call handling, caller disclosures, booking workflow, integrations and staff handoff. Confirm each capability directly with the vendor.
