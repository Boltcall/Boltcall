# Boltcall product marketing context

Updated 2026-10-01. Source: docs/law-firm-pivot-plan.md (decisions locked 2026-08-15), CLAUDE.md, canonical pricing.

## Positioning
- Category: speed-to-lead software for law firms (legal intake). Not "AI receptionist" as the headline.
- Promise: the first firm to call a potential client back signs the case. Boltcall answers and books every new inquiry in seconds, 24/7.
- ICP (beachhead): solo and small to mid-size law firms. General practice messaging; practice areas shown: personal injury, family, criminal defense, immigration, estate planning.
- Other verticals: code stays multi-industry, but they stay out of marketing messaging. Routes stay alive.

## Pricing (monthly, canonical)
Starter $549, Pro $897, Ultimate $4,997, Enterprise $997 (custom). Flat subscription, shared monthly credit pool, free setup, 30-day money-back guarantee (Terms section 7). No per-case fees, no bonus per signed case, no share of fees (ABA Model Rule 5.4 risk).
ROI framing: signed-case value (placeholder $2,500, src/data/industryDefaults.ts), shown as arithmetic with the visitor's own fee. Never forecast results.

## Claims we can make
- Answers inbound calls, missed calls, forms, and texts within seconds, 24/7.
- Opens every call by saying it is an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. Cannot be switched off.
- Intake only. Never gives legal advice, assesses a case, predicts value, or quotes fees unless told to.
- Flags urgent matters (custody, arrest, 48h court date, deportation, imminent deadline) and alerts the team.
- Collects conflict-check names first. Says the call does not make the caller a client. Never promises confidentiality.
- Books into Google Calendar and Cal.com. Other tools via Zapier, Make, webhooks. No native Clio/MyCase/Filevine.
- English and Spanish.

## Claims we must not make (unverifiable)
Testimonials, star ratings, customer counts, "most customers see X", case studies, response-time guarantees (e.g. "11 seconds"), third-party stats without a linkable source.

## Voice
Noam: story-led, direct, no fluff. No em dashes. No hashtags. Do not invent customers, stats, or quotes.
